A model, not a whole engine

One cylinder and four strokes, drawn to show the idea. Parts are not to scale. Colour suggests changing conditions, not measured temperature. Real valve timing, camshaft, lubrication and starter are left out.

The crank and rod show the motion connection, not a calculation of pressure, efficiency or whether a real engine would start.
